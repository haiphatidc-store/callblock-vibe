CallBlock Vibe

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · Developer: Hai Phat LTD

Hai Phat LTD ("we," "us," "our") built CallBlock Vibe (the "App") to identify incoming callers, block spam calls, and provide a simple dialer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, what is processed only on your device, and what is shared with third-party services the App relies on.

1. Information the App accesses on your device

To provide caller identification, spam blocking, and related calling features, the App requests the following Android permissions. The data these permissions expose is processed locally on your device to power the corresponding feature and is not uploaded to any server operated by us:

PermissionWhy the App needs it
READ_PHONE_STATEDetect an incoming/outgoing call so the App can show caller ID and call-state information.
READ_CALL_LOGDisplay your call history inside the App.
READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTSMatch incoming numbers against your saved contacts and let you add or edit contacts from the App.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow notifications such as spam-call alerts and missed-call alerts.
ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS, CALL_PHONE, MANAGE_OWN_CALLSLet the App act as a dialer/in-call screen: answer, place, and manage calls.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALLKeep the in-call screen and call-screening service running reliably while a call is active.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENTShow the full-screen incoming-call UI, similar to the stock dialer.
WAKE_LOCKKeep the device processor active long enough to handle an incoming call or screening decision.

The App also includes a Call Screening Service that evaluates incoming numbers on-device to identify or block spam calls, an In-Call Service that powers the dialer and in-call screen when you set the App as your default or an additional calling app, and a Notification Listener Service used for the App's "Unseen" feature: it reads the preview text of notifications you already received (it does not mark them as read on the original app) so the App can show you a consolidated view of recent notifications. This processing happens entirely on your device; notification content read for this feature is not transmitted to us or stored on any server.

None of your call log, contacts, dialed numbers, or notification preview content is uploaded to servers operated by Hai Phat LTD. This data stays on your device and is used only to power the features described above.

2. Third-party services used by the App

The App uses the following third-party services, which operate independently of the on-device processing described above. These services may collect device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID) and usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and its mediation partners — Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Pangle (TikTok for Business), Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff Monetize), and Mintegral — are integrated in the App to serve and measure advertising. These networks may receive your Advertising ID and other device identifiers in accordance with Google's and each network's own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — collects app usage and interaction data (such as screens viewed and features used) to help us understand how the App is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — collects crash reports and diagnostic logs to help us find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config / Firebase Realtime Database (Google) — used to remotely configure app behavior and sync non-personal configuration data.
  • Adjust — an attribution service that helps us understand which channel brought you to the App. Adjust may receive your Advertising ID and device/network information for this purpose. See Adjust's Privacy Policy.

These services initialize automatically when the App starts, independent of whether you have interacted with any advertising inside the App.

3. Your choices

  • You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID used for ad personalization at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by Android version).
  • You can revoke any Android permission listed above at any time in Settings > Apps > CallBlock Vibe > Permissions. Revoking a permission disables the corresponding feature (for example, revoking Phone permissions disables caller ID and call screening) but does not affect other parts of the App.
  • You can turn off notification access for the App (disabling the "Unseen" feature) in Settings > Apps > Special app access > Notification access.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which removes all app-local data from your device.

4. Data retention

Call log, contact matches, and notification previews processed for the features above are kept only on your device for as long as the App or Android itself retains them, and are deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We do not operate a server that stores this content.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility app (caller ID, spam blocking, dialer) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

6. Security

We rely on Android's built-in app sandboxing and the security practices of the third-party services listed above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after a change becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
