FAQ
Is CallBlock Vibe free?

Yes. Every feature is free and unlocked. There is no sign-up and no in-app purchase to unlock anything.

Where does caller identification happen?

On your device. Caller ID and spam risk are computed from your call log and contacts locally; that data is not uploaded to our servers.

Why does it need call and contacts permissions?

To show caller ID on incoming calls, screen blocked numbers, display your call history, and manage contacts. These are the core phone features of the app.

What is the “Unseen” feature?

It lets you preview new message notifications without marking them as read. It uses notification access and processes previews on your device.

What languages are supported?

15 languages, covering the whole app from the dialer to settings.
